# Story Index

This is the **production plan** — what we draft, in what order, and where each unit
stands. The authoritative book-by-book catalogue of the whole canon (every unit,
with descriptions, links, keywords, and `I·C·P·H·A` scores) now lives in **`R1`**;
this file no longer tries to duplicate it. What `06` holds that `R1` doesn't: the
**calibration set** we tackle first, the **draft order**, and live **status**. The
Genesis breakdown below is kept as the worked planning model, but for the canonical
catalogue and scores, `R1` is the source of truth.

Pick draft order with the `R0` scores (see the Operation Guide): narrative sequence
first, and within it reach for the high **I + P** units a newcomer most needs.

Status key: ⬜ not started · 🟨 drafting · ✅ reviewed · 🔒 locked

## A. The shape of the whole (framework)

Rough scale: the full canon yields on the order of **1,000+ units**. We don't
enumerate them all yet — we lock the format first, then expand this section book
by book in the order below.

**Suggested production order (Phase 4):**
1. **Torah** — Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy
2. **Gospels & Acts** — high familiarity, high payoff
3. **Historical books** — Joshua, Judges, Ruth, Samuel, Kings, Chronicles, Ezra–Nehemiah, Esther
4. **Wisdom & poetry** — Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Songs
5. **Prophets** — Isaiah … Malachi
6. **Epistles** — Romans … Jude
7. **Revelation**
8. *(if canon scope expands)* **Deuterocanon** — Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach, Maccabees, etc.
